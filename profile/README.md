# Household Hub

**The operating system of your household.**

## Goal

The goal of Household Hub is to provide a centralized platform for managing household-related information, tasks, inventories, documents, and routines in a simple and user-friendly way.

The project aims to help individuals and families reduce administrative overhead, improve organization, and maintain a clear overview of their household operations.

## Vision

Our vision is to build an open, extensible, and privacy-conscious household management ecosystem that becomes the central hub for everyday home administration.

Household Hub should enable users to seamlessly organize their homes, automate repetitive processes, and access all relevant household information from a single place, while maintaining full ownership and control of their data.
